# SwarmPay cover artwork

Original vector artwork, created for this review on November 10, 2026. No generated image model was used: the built-in generator was unavailable and the connected image tools returned an unavailable-tool error. The image-generation skill has an API fallback that requires OPENAI_API_KEY and explicit authorization; it was not used.

## Creative brief
Create a bright, confident cover for SwarmPay. Preserve its four rounded tiles and evergreen identity. Use a vivid citron field, deep green typography and sky-blue secondary planes. Show coordinated merchant work through two illustrated tiles, tents and catering, connected to a sheet representing approved terms. The sheet's check symbolizes approval; it is not a payment receipt, certification or proof of real fulfillment. Avoid robots, crypto coins, fake dashboards, third-party logos and unverified claims.

Exact cover copy: “SwarmPay”; “Agent commerce.”; “On your terms.”; “AI plans. You stay in control.”; “swarm.decionis.com”. The 3:2 master also says “One budget.” and “Two merchants. Your say.”

## Exports
- Devpost: 1800 x 1200 PNG and editable vector SVG (3:2). Devpost recommends 3:2 with a 5 MB maximum; PNG size is checked in the manifest.
- Wide: 1920 x 1080 PNG and SVG (16:9), composed separately for a video title or presentation cover.
- Social: 1200 x 630 PNG and SVG, composed separately for link previews.
- Brand palette: 1800 x 1180 PNG and SVG.
- Payment-state specimen: 1600 x 780 PNG and SVG; explicitly a design specification, not a live screenshot.

The wide and social exports are adapted compositions from the same artwork, not crops of the Devpost master. SVG lettering is outlined for consistent rendering; labels remain in the SVG metadata and the editable fonts are supplied. The geometry is fully vector. The four-tile mark preserves the source project's form; this is a proposed color treatment, not a trademark clearance.
