# Event-operator research protocol

Prepared November 10, 2026. **Participants interviewed: 0. Findings recorded: 0.** No outreach has been sent. The initial audience is office and community organizers coordinating tent rentals and catering. Broader procurement is an unvalidated expansion.

## Recruit and obtain consent

The owner must nominate three actual organizers and authorize contact. Include at least one office event and one community event, each with recent responsibility for two or more suppliers. Ask for a 25-minute conversation about a completed event, permission to take anonymized notes and a retention preference. Do not request payment credentials, attendee lists, contracts containing personal data or sensitive event briefs. Recording requires separate consent.

## Interview guide

1. Walk through the last event you organized: guest count, suppliers, lead time and who approved the budget.
2. What happened between the first brief and an agreed supplier plan? Ask for the actual elapsed time and deliberate organizer actions; distinguish recall from a timed observation.
3. When did each supplier require payment? How did you know the delivery matched the order, and who could resolve disagreement?
4. Describe the last changed order, cancellation, failed payment or disputed delivery. What did you do, and how long did the exception stay open?
5. Show the clearly labeled SwarmPay demonstration. Ask the participant to explain plan consent, the two PayPal approvals, an authorization, a held request and the venue's signed assertion in their own words.
6. Which step would they trust an agent to propose? Which should require a person? What missing supplier or venue integration prevents use?
7. Ask how they would choose or pay for this service, who owns that budget and what evidence a paid pilot would require. Record the answer without inventing a price or treating hypothetical interest as a purchase.

Do not lead with “Would this save you time?” or promise autonomous negotiation, escrow, guaranteed delivery, hosted enforcement or proven ROI.

## Measurement plan

Compare the participant's actual baseline process with an observed, consented prototype task of comparable scope. Record sample size, exclusions, task completion and the environment. The current `/api/measurements` export provides successful server planning time, session brief-to-plan time, HUMAN audit actions, held requests, receipt-to-capture time and resolved/unresolved exceptions. Definitions and limits are in the [technical review](../technical-review.md#measured-behavior).

Prototype latency is not time saved. Callbacks are not clicks. A signed receipt is not physical-delivery verification. Report medians/ranges only after real observations exist, include failures and label simulated payments. Customer impact needs the baseline and comparable task evidence.
