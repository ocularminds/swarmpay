# Kintlo artwork direction

Adapted on November 10, 2026 from the original vector artwork created for the SwarmPay review. Kintlo is the owner-selected commercial name. Preserve the four-tile form, evergreen identity, Manrope display type and DM Sans interface type. Use bright citron for the cover, deep ink for legible type, and sky for supporting illustrations.

The illustrated tent and catering tiles represent the current event use case. A connecting path and an approved-terms sheet express coordinated work under the organizer's control. The check symbolizes approved terms; it is not a certification, guarantee of fulfillment, or payment receipt. The artwork makes no claim about an award, commercial launch, independent audit or registered domain.

## Exact proposed copy

- Name: **Kintlo**.
- Hero: **Many agents.** / **Your rules.**
- Supporting line: **AI plans. You stay in control.**
- Category descriptor: **Agent commerce**.
- Project cover only: **One plan.** / **Every payment on your terms.**

The name was selected by the owner; the brand line and its application remain proposed creative. Do not insert `kintlo.com` into artwork or metadata until domain ownership and the intended launch URL have been verified.

## Export treatment

Project, wide and social covers are separately composed at 1800 x 1200, 1920 x 1080 and 1200 x 630 pixels. They are not automatic crops. The palette is 1800 x 1180; the state specimen is 1600 x 780; the wordmark is 680 x 160. Keep all lettering inside safe margins and dark on bright backgrounds.

SVG lettering is outlined for consistent display; accessible labels and text metadata remain available. Fonts and their SIL Open Font Licenses accompany the kit. All PNGs are deterministic renders of these vectors, not AI-generated raster images.

## Provenance and usage

The geometric artwork and four-tile treatment come from this conversation's original SwarmPay design kit, which referenced the project's own mark and typography at source commit `10c58a8`. No third-party photos, logos, music or stock illustrations are used. The new kit does not modify the original submission assets or the running application.

Contrast calculations carry over unchanged because the color values are unchanged. They are color-pair measurements, not a complete accessibility audit. Check the implemented interface separately.
