**Deliberate scope limits**

Organizers sign up with PayPal or use the configured password account; USD; two merchants, with a curated catalog of rentals and caterers and Channel3 listings for tents to buy, which are paid to the sandbox tents merchant rather than ordered from the listing store; one catering milestone; exact full-amount captures. UI event input uses the browser timezone, and stored/MCP event timestamps carry an explicit IANA timezone. Agents provide structured recommendations and summaries; they do not browse arbitrary stores or control payments directly, and the Channel3 search runs on the server. Prices include simulated buyer taxes/delivery, capacity is a fixture, and rain is organizer-provided context.

Plans may be prepared within 28 days, and catering may be authorized up to 27 days before the event: a PayPal authorization lasts 29 days, with funds guaranteed for its first 3. Once that honor period has passed and the event is within 3 days, the worker reauthorizes the catering hold once (Payments v2 reauthorize, through AgentSafe), and the delivery capture uses the renewed authorization; PayPal itself refuses a reauthorization inside the honor period. A live reauthorization needs an authorization at least 4 days old, so it has been exercised with HTTP fixtures, not yet in the sandbox. Quotes expire after 30 minutes. Changed terms require a newly approved plan; automatic supersession of an already funded plan is not supported. Pending/unknown operations keep their commitments reserved. Voiding an authorization on its own does not release the reservation; cancelling the event does, once the voids and refunds have settled.

Payment holds remain holds. There is no application button that overrides a Decionis BLOCK or ESCALATE. The local retry action is available only after confirmed non-dispatch and obtains a fresh authority decision using the same command identity. Hosted Presence resume is an outstanding integration, not a completed feature.
